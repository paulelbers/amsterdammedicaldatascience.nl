Paul ElbersMD, PhD, EDIC

Intensivist and associate professor of intensive care medicine, Amsterdam UMC

p.elbers@amsterdamumc.nl
amsterdammedicaldatascience.nl/paulelbers
ORCID 0000-0003-0447-6893

Intensivist with a background in cardiac anesthesia, working where intensive care medicine meets data science: sharing intensive care data, building models with artificial intelligence, bringing them to the bedside as decision support, and testing in clinical trials whether patients are better off. Co-founder of AmsterdamUMCdb, the first freely available intensive care database from within the European Union, and founding director of the Center for Critical Care Computational Intelligence at Amsterdam UMC. Current focus: reinforcement learning at the bedside, with randomization when needed.
